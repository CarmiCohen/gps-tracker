## ✅ Resolved Issues (Nov7.21)
- **The Sensor Reports A Real GPS Loss (ISSUE-NOV7.19-O43)**: `SensorService.lastLocation` was set by each fix and never aged, so after a real signal loss the sensing loop kept sending the last fix with a fresh timestamp and the Monitor's `Tracker GPS signal loss` could only fire before the Sensor's first fix. Reporting `NO_FIX` needed a transport change first, because `SocketioSignalingClient.sendTelemetry` and `SignalingQueue` treated every status other than `VALID` as critical (unbatched, never dropped, not deduplicated), which would have flooded the relay at 10 Hz. **SIMP-NOV7.19-S1, implemented**: `LocationUpdate.isCritical` (`core-shared/.../Models.kt`, cleared by `reset()`, not put on the wire) now drives all four decisions: the unbatched send in `sendTelemetry` and the deduplication, `pushFront`, and drop-oldest rules in `SignalingQueue`. `SensorService` sets it only on an update carrying a `JUMP` or `OUTLIER` verdict and passes it to `ForensicBuffer.writeTrace`, which commits such an entry at once. **Loss rule**: the new `app-sensor/.../FixFreshness.kt` holds `FIX_STALE_AFTER_MS` (15 s, three times the 5 s location request) and `isFixFresh` on `elapsedRealtimeNanos`; while the Sensor has no fix younger than that, each tick is sent as `NO_FIX` with no position, and the service logs `No current GPS fix ... reporting NO_FIX` and `GPS fix current again` at each change. **Found live and fixed in the same version**: after location was switched back on, the A15's first fix was a cell fix 3352 m off at 700 m accuracy, reported as `JUMP: 97.5 m/s, 3352 m in 34.4 s, accuracy 13 to 700 m`, and the GPS fix after it as `OUTLIER 488 m/s`, on a phone lying still; `MovementCheck` now neither judges nor keeps as its reference a fix without accuracy or coarser than `MOVEMENT_CHECK_MAX_ACCURACY_M` (100 m). Verified by 13 new unit tests (`FixFreshnessTest` 5, `SignalingQueueTest` +4, `SignalingBatchTest` +1 with `testCriticalEventFlushesBatch` moved to `isCritical`, `MovementCheckTest` +3), 189 of 189 unit tests, `ProductionReadinessAuditTest` 3 of 3 on the A15, the device audit (0 Davey, StackLog, or fatal entries in both apps), and live on the A15 through the production relay: `NO_FIX` at 20:37:59 about 15 s after the last fix with location off, `GPS fix current again` 1.9 s after it was back on, no violation on recovery after the fix (20:41:35), and with the Monitor running the Monitor stored 126 contiguous `NO_FIX` rows (sequences 3314 to 3439) with no position, and raised no alarm for the 13 s gap. The Monitor's 30 s `Tracker GPS signal loss` on `NO_FIX` was not sounded live; it is covered by `MonitorAlarmsTest`. New gap: ISSUE-NOV7.21-O45 (a Sensor start sounds a false `Power disconnected` siren). SOT rule 1.376, test chapter 140. **PASSED.**

## ✅ Resolved Issues (Nov7.19)
- **No False Alarms At Start (ISSUE-NOV7.17-O42)**: every Monitor cold start raised `RELAY_DISCONNECTED` and sounded the siren at full volume, and a start of both apps also raised `GPS_SIGNAL_LOSS` and `GPS_JUMP`. Three root causes, all confirmed in the A15 log of 2026-11-07 19:46: (1) `SocketioSignalingClient.isConnected` starts `false`, and `alarmBooks` raised the relay alarm on that first value, 18 ms after `Connecting to relay`; the relay alarm now holds only after the relay was connected once in this watch, or 30 s (`RELAY_FIRST_CONNECT_GRACE_MS`) after watching starts with no connection. (2) The Sensor sends `(0,0)` until its first fix, and the Monitor raised GPS loss on the first such reading; `GPS_SIGNAL_LOSS` now needs 30 s (`GPS_SIGNAL_LOSS_AFTER_MS`) without a position, tracked as `noGpsSignalSinceMs`. (3) `SensorService` ran `validateMovement` on every loop tick (100 ms while charging) against the previous tick, so the drift between two fixes about 5 s apart was divided by one tick: the network fix (82.5 m) to GPS fix (14.3 m) correction of 34.5 m became `OUTLIER 346 m/s`. The new `MovementCheck` (`app-sensor/.../MovementCheck.kt`) checks once per new fix, timed by `Location.elapsedRealtimeNanos`. A still A15 then showed `JUMP: 14.3 m/s, 23 m in 1.6 s, accuracy 68 to 59 m`, so `SentinelLogic.validateMovement` now treats a move smaller than the larger accuracy as `VALID` (`Within GPS error`), which replaces the narrower `Suppressed Accuracy Snap` rule, and `JUMP` reasons carry their measurements. Found on the way: `SensorService` built the `ViolationEvent` inside `serviceScope.launch`, after the pooled update had been released and zeroed, so violations were sent with an empty location; it is now built before the launch. `lastLocation` is `@Volatile`. Verified by 14 new unit tests (`MonitorAlarmsTest` +6, `MovementCheckTest` 6, `SentinelLogicTest` +2), 176 of 176 unit tests, `ProductionReadinessAuditTest` 3 of 3 on the A15, and live on the A15: Monitor cold start and a start of both apps with no alarm, siren, or violation over 48 s and 85 s, and 0 violations over 2 minutes on a still phone (Nov7.17: 4 in 90 s). New gaps: ISSUE-NOV7.19-O43 (the Sensor never reports a real GPS loss) and ISSUE-NOV7.19-O44 (`PermissionGuard` does not re-check on resume, split out of O42). SOT rule 1.375 (rules 1.366 and 1.368 amended), test chapter 139. **PASSED.**

## ✅ Resolved Issues (Nov7.15)
- **Backfill Answer Stops On Send Failure (ISSUE-NOV6.4-O33)**: `SensorService.setupBackfillListener` ignored the result of `sendBackfillResponse`, so after a connection drop between chunks the completion chunk could still reach the Monitor, which then marked the range answered with rows missing until the Monitor process restarted. The chunk loop moved to the new `answerBackfill` in `app-sensor/.../BackfillResponder.kt` (`BACKFILL_CHUNK_SIZE` = 50), which stops at the first failed send and never sends a later chunk or the completion flag for that request; the service logs the failure. The Monitor was not changed: an incomplete request is released by `BackfillLedger` after 30 s without chunks, and the next stored-trail gap scan asks for the rows still missing (rule 1.361). Verified by the 7 tests in the new `BackfillResponderTest` (the first JVM unit test in `:app-sensor`) and by all unit tests passing across all subprojects. SOT rule 1.373, test chapter 137. **PASSED.**
