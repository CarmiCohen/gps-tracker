## ✅ Resolved Issues (Nov9.0-LiftTilt)
- **Tilt And Lift Alarms (ISSUE-NOV5.2-O11 Group 5, High)**: the `Tilt Angle Detection Sensor` (with its sensitivity slider) and `Lift / Height Change Sensor` switches existed, but nothing detected tilt or lift. **Finding**: the A15 has no barometer, no gyroscope, and no gravity sensor (`dumpsys sensorservice`: accelerometer, magnetometer, light, proximity, Samsung virtual sensors). **User decisions (2026-11-09)**: lift by accelerometer pickup on every phone, plus the barometer on phones that have one; the Sensor uses its own tilt slider; the gravity defect below is fixed in this version.
  - **Detection** (`core-shared/MotionSentinel.kt`): gravity is a 0.3 s low-pass of the accelerometer; a sample farther than 0.35 m/s² from it is motion. After 30 s of rest (`MOTION_ARM_AFTER_STILL_MS`) tilt and lift are armed with the resting gravity as reference. **Tilt**: the gravity direction differs from the reference by at least `tiltThresholdForSensitivity(slider)` = 25° − 20° × slider (0.5 gives the old spec's 15°, 1.0 gives 5°) for 0.5 s. **Lift (accelerometer)**: from the first motion, the acceleration along the current gravity direction minus the resting gravity is integrated twice for at most 3 s (ending after 0.3 s of rest); 20 cm up alerts. Projecting on the gravity direction keeps a sideways shove out (a first version used the magnitude, which a hard sideways push raises), and the resting magnitude cancels the sensor offset. **Lift (barometer)**: the pressure, smoothed over 2 s, moves 0.8 m of height (standard atmosphere) away from a reference that follows over 120 s (weather) for 2 s (door slams). An alert disarms; the next 30 s rest re-arms at the new place, so one disturbance gives one alert. A sensor pause over 200 ms resets the integration.
  - **Sensor** (`SensorService.kt`): the accelerometer now runs at `SENSOR_DELAY_GAME` (about 50 Hz, was `NORMAL`, about 5 Hz) on a `MotionSensors` thread, the barometer at the normal rate where present. The tilt angle follows the Sensor's stored `tiltSensitivity`. Tilt, lift, and noise alerts go through one queue; the next sensing tick sends `TILT_ALERT` or `LIFT_ALERT` (`Models.kt`) as a critical telemetry status and a violation event, through a new `reportViolation` helper that replaced the three copies of that code (part of SIMP-NOV8.103-S1). The Sensor screen and Setup dialog show `motionStatusText` (`Tilt & Lift: Off`, `Arms after the phone rests 30 s`, `Armed (accelerometer)` or `(accelerometer + barometer)`); the debug `STATE` answer now adds `mic=`, `motionArmed=`, and `barometer=`.
  - **Gravity defect fixed**: `SensorService` passed the `TYPE_GRAVITY` values to `rotateToWorldSpace`, which returns 0 for a zero vector; on the A15, which has no gravity sensor, `vibration` was therefore always 0 and the GPS movement check never saw physical motion (`hasPhysicalMotion` always false). Phones without a gravity sensor now use the `MotionSentinel` estimate, so jump verdicts on the A15 can now count physical motion.
  - **Monitor** (`MonitorAlarms.kt`): `Tracker tilted` (`tiltAlert`) and `Tracker lifted` (`liftAlert`); the violation statuses with an alarm of their own are now one map, `SENSOR_VIOLATION_ALARMS`. The Monitor's Alert Settings show a note instead of the tilt slider (`TILT_SENSITIVITY_ON_SENSOR_NOTE`); on the Sensor the slider is always shown.
  - **Tests**: `MotionSentinelTest` (15: arming, tilt over and under the limit, slider, 32 cm pickup, 9.6 cm rise, knock and shove, re-arming, gravity estimate and the vibration it gives, sensor pause, barometer 1 m, weather drift, door slam, altitude formula), `MotionAlarmTest` (5; the switched-off case also unplugs the row, so the power alarm proves the row was evaluated), `SensorServiceStatusTest` (+1). ## ✅ Resolved Issues (Nov8.103-Microphone)
- **Microphone Noise Alarm (ISSUE-NOV5.2-O11 Group 4, High)**: the `Acoustic / Microphone Noise Sensor` switch (`AlertSettings.acousticAlert`) existed, but no app used a microphone. **User decisions (2026-11-08)**: only the Sensor listens and sends events, the Monitor never opens a microphone; the Monitor switch only gates the alarm (the Sensor listens whenever it holds the permission); the trigger is a jump of 30 dB above the learned background.
  - **Detection** (`core-shared/AcousticDetector.kt`): `acousticLevelDbfs` turns each 100 ms frame of 16-bit samples into dBFS (a phone microphone measures relative to full scale, not sound pressure), or null for an all-zero frame (Android delivers zeros for a silenced microphone). `AcousticDetector` learns the background (3 s warm-up; then it follows quieter frames within about 1 s and louder frames over about 10 s, so a lasting noise becomes background) and alerts on a jump of `ACOUSTIC_JUMP_DB` = 30 dB that is at least -50 dBFS, at most once per 10 s.
  - **Sensor** (`AcousticMonitor.kt`, `SensorService.kt`, manifest): an `AudioRecord` thread (16 kHz mono, `VOICE_RECOGNITION` source, which has no automatic gain) feeds the detector; no audio is stored or sent. `RECORD_AUDIO` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE` are declared and `SensorService` declares and requests the `microphone` service type when the permission is granted. Android 14+ refuses that type to a service started in the background (after a reboot): the service then runs without it, shows `Microphone: Open the Sensor app to start listening`, and `AdminActivity` sends a new start command when the app is opened, which takes the microphone. An alert sets a pending note and preempts the sensing loop, whose next tick sends status `ACOUSTIC_ALERT_STATUS` = `ACOUSTIC_ALERT` with the current position as a critical telemetry row and a `violation_event` (a tick that already carries a violation keeps it; the noise follows on the next tick).
  - **Permission and status**: `RECORD_AUDIO` is asked with the required permissions but is optional (`OPTIONAL_MICROPHONE_PERMISSION`). The Sensor console and Setup dialog show `MicrophoneState` via `microphoneStatusText` (`Off`, `Audio Denied`, `Open the Sensor app to start listening`, `Listening`, `Silenced by the system` after 5 s of zeros, `Not available`), with a `GRANT MICROPHONE` button when denied.
  - **Monitor** (`MonitorAlarms.kt`): new `AlarmType.ACOUSTIC_ALERT` (`Tracker noise detected`), allowed by `acousticAlert`, raised by the telemetry status or the violation type. The violation conditions were reorganized: geofence breach and noise are independent, and only violations without an alarm of their own count as a GPS jump (before, any non-geofence violation event was a GPS jump).
  - **Tests**: `AcousticDetectorTest` (12), `AcousticAlarmTest` (6), `MonitorMicrophoneGuardTest` (2: no microphone class or permission in `app-monitor`, the Sensor's declared), `SensorServiceStatusTest` (+2). `run_tests.ps1 test assembleDebug assembleRelease`: 221 tests passed, no new warning (the 20 warning lines are ISSUE-NOV8.13-O57 and `CleanSetupActionsTest`). One own test expectation was wrong (a 29.9 dB jump) and was corrected. A15: `ProductionReadinessAuditTest` 3 of 3 (via `am instrument`, which force-stops the Sensor; restored by opening it), device audit 0 Davey, StackLog, or fatal entries in both apps; live: `Microphone: Audio Denied` with the button after denying, `Microphone: Listening` after granting through the button, service types `0x289` (with microphone). The Sensor's location and notification permissions were found not granted on the A15 before this session and were granted with `pm grant`. **Not run**: a real noise near the A15 (the user was away; deferred, chapter 161.4). On a phone running both apps, the Monitor siren can be heard by the Sensor microphone; the alarm stays one pending alarm until acknowledged.
