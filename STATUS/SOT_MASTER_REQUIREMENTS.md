*   **1.304 Forensic UI Jitter Audit (R-ID 771)**: The `MonitorRepository` MUST utilize `OnConflictStrategy.REPLACE` and standardized sequence ID ordering to ensure smooth trail rendering during high-frequency backfill ingestion, preventing visual "jumping" or duplicate polyline segments. (Revol31.0 - ISSUE-REVOL30.0-F7).
*   **1.305 Forensic Buffer Spill Audit (R-ID 773)**: The `ForensicBuffer` MUST implement proactive disk space verification and robust I/O recovery to prevent telemetry loss during storage-full conditions. Rollover integrity MUST be maintained under sustained 50Hz burst pressure. (Revol31.1 - ISSUE-REVOL30.0-F8).
*   **1.306 Signaling Resilience Hardening (R-ID 772)**: The `SocketioSignalingClient` MUST employ an aggressive reconnection strategy (`Int.MAX_VALUE` attempts) and an exhaustive queue flushing mechanism that guarantees all queued telemetry is transmitted immediately upon reconnection, ensuring zero data loss during LTE/WiFi flapping. (Revol31.2 - ISSUE-REVOL30.1-F2).
*   **1.307 Socket Testability (R-ID 774)**: The signaling infrastructure MUST utilize a `SocketFactory` abstraction and injectable `IoDispatcher` to allow deterministic unit testing of connection state logic and telemetry flushing without reliance on live network interfaces. (Revol31.2 - ISSUE-REVOL30.1-F2).
*   **1.308 Geofence Transition Forensic Integrity (R-ID 775)**: The `SentinelLogic` MUST detect `ENTER`/`EXIT` transitions with nanosecond precision, and the `ForensicBuffer` MUST persist these events alongside their `monotonicNanos` and `status` strings to ensure a complete, non-repudiable forensic trail of spatial violations. (Revol31.8 - ISSUE-REVOL30.1-F3).
*   **1.309 Signaling Pipeline Soak Integrity (R-ID 776)**: The `SignalingQueue` MUST implement critical event prioritization and support at least 5000 in-memory events to guarantee zero telemetry loss during extended network disconnections at 10Hz. All "ENTER/EXIT" and "JUMP" status events MUST be protected from queue eviction during overflow conditions. (Revol31.9 - ISSUE-REVOL31.9-F1).
*   **1.310 FGS Permission & API 34 Security (R-ID 10)**: All Foreground Services MUST implement strict runtime permission guards before calling `startForeground` to prevent `SecurityException` on API 34+. Specifically, `location` types MUST verify `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` or `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` and include appropriate type bits (`LOCATION`, `DATA_SYNC`, `REMOTE_MESSAGING`) to match manifest declarations and operational needs. (Nov1.0 - ISSUE-REVOL31.9-F2).
*   **1.311 Backfill Recovery Contention Audit (R-ID 80)**: The `MonitorRepository` MUST implement range-based request deduplication to prevent redundant backfill requests during concurrent telemetry ingestion. Telemetry ingestion MUST ensure zero duplicate records in the `TrailDao` even during multi-peer contention or overlapping backfill responses. (Nov1.1 - ISSUE-REVOL32.0-F1).
*   **1.312 Forensic Signaling Batching (R-ID 81)**: The `SignalingClient` MUST implement adaptive telemetry batching (time and size based) to optimize packet overhead during high-frequency bursts or recovery. *   **1.313 Adaptive Telemetry Sampling (R-ID 82)**: The `SensorService` MUST implement velocity-based adaptive sampling to optimize battery life. The `TickOrchestrator` MUST support preemption to ensure immediate adaptation to velocity changes. (Nov1.3 - ISSUE-REVOL34.0-F1).
*   **1.314 Battery-Aware Thermal Throttling (R-ID 83)**: The `SensingEngine` MUST enforce multi-stage resource constraints. Sensing intervals MUST be capped at 1Hz during thermal warning (>40°C) or low battery (<20%), and 0.1Hz during critical thermal (>45°C) or critical battery (<15%). These constraints MUST override velocity-based performance modes to prevent device shutdown and ensure forensic continuity. (Nov1.4 - ISSUE-REVOL35.0-F1).

(Nov1.5 - ISSUE-REVOL36.0-F1).

*   **1.365 Monitor Alarms And Siren (R-ID 136)**: While a tracker is paired, the Monitor MUST raise an alarm when the tracker sends nothing for 35 seconds, reports no charging, reports a battery below 20%, or reports a temperature above 46°C, each only while its Alert Settings switch is on and Global Mute is off. *   **1.367 30-Second Siren Auto-Stop (Timer or Acknowledge) (R-ID 138)**: The Monitor siren MUST automatically stop after 30 seconds (`30s`) or when acknowledged, whichever occurs first. *   **1.368 False MOVING Fix & Sensor-Detected Violations (R-ID 139)**: The Sensor MUST run `SentinelLogic.validateMovement` with accelerometer motion (`hasPhysicalMotion`) once on each new fix (rule 1.375) to suppress GPS drift and false MOVING while stationary, and MUST transmit its motion state and violation status (`JUMP`, `OUTLIER`) in telemetry and via `ViolationEvent`. The Monitor MUST raise a GPS jump alarm (`AlarmType.GPS_JUMP`) when a violation or jump status is received, following `AlertSettings.jammerDetection` (R-ID 139).
*   **1.369 HUD Flow Rate-Limiting (R-ID 140)**: The Monitor `localHudState` and `remoteHudState` flows, as well as the Sensor `hudState` flow, MUST apply `atUiRate()` (500ms sampling window) to incoming telemetry and fix streams, capping UI state recalculations and emissions to at most 2Hz to prevent excess CPU usage, unnecessary IPC calls, and rapid recomposition (R-ID 140).
*   **1.370 Screen State & Rotation Preservation (R-ID 141)**: All UI overlay visibility flags, dialog states, and map follow modes in `MonitorActivity` and `AdminActivity` MUST use `rememberSaveable` to ensure open overlays, dialogs, unlock states, and follow modes persist across screen rotation and configuration changes (R-ID 141).
*   **1.371 Thread-Safe Signaling Queue & Order-Preserving Failed Flush (R-ID 142)**: The signaling queue (`SignalingQueue`) MUST perform all queue manipulations and sequence tracking under a `ReentrantLock` for multi-threaded safety between telemetry offering and queue flushing. When a batch flush fails due to a network disconnection or transmit failure in `SocketioSignalingClient`, the un-sent updates MUST be re-queued at the front of the queue (`pushFront`) in their exact original sequence order to prevent reordering behind newer incoming updates (R-ID 142).
*   **1.372 Coarse Network Fix Filtering (R-ID 143)**: `MonitorLocationSource` MUST filter incoming location updates using `isBetterLocation` to prevent a coarse Network location fix (e.g., accuracy ±77.6m) from replacing a fresh, more accurate GPS location fix (e.g., accuracy ±11m) on the Monitor UI and map. A coarse Network fix MUST only replace a GPS fix if the GPS fix is older than 2 minutes (120s) (R-ID 143).
*   **1.373 Backfill Answer Stops On Send Failure (R-ID 144)**: The Sensor MUST answer a backfill request in chunks of at most 50 rows and MUST stop at the first chunk that `sendBackfillResponse` fails to send. After a failed chunk it MUST NOT send any later chunk of that request, including the completion chunk (`isComplete`), so the Monitor never counts a range with missing rows as answered; the Monitor's 30-second request release and stored-trail gap scan (rule 1.361) then request the remaining rows (ISSUE-NOV6.4-O33) (R-ID 144).
*   **1.374 Real Sensor Service Status (R-ID 145)**: The Sensor Setup dialog MUST show the service state reported by `SensorService` itself through the `@Singleton` `SensorServiceStatus` (running only after `initializeServiceComponents` has started the service components, stopped again in `onDestroy`) and the relay state from `SignalingClient.isConnected`, as `Sensor Service: Running` / `Stopped` and `Relay Connection: Connected` / `Disconnected`. *   **1.375 No False Alarms At Start (R-ID 146)**: The Monitor MUST NOT raise `Relay disconnected` for a relay it has not yet connected to until 30 seconds after it starts watching (`RELAY_FIRST_CONNECT_GRACE_MS`); after a connection, a lost relay MUST raise it at once. `Tracker GPS signal loss` MUST be raised only after the tracker has reported no position for 30 seconds (`GPS_SIGNAL_LOSS_AFTER_MS`). The Sensor MUST check movement once per new fix, against the previous fix and timed by the fixes' monotonic clock, never on loop ticks that send the same fix again; `SentinelLogic.validateMovement` MUST treat a move smaller than the larger of the two fixes' accuracies as `VALID`, and a `JUMP` reason MUST carry the measured speed, distance, interval, and accuracies. A `ViolationEvent` MUST be built before its send is launched, from a copy of the update (ISSUE-NOV7.17-O42) (R-ID 146).
*   **1.376 Real GPS Loss Reporting & Explicit Critical Delivery (R-ID 147)**: Whether a telemetry update is sent unbatched, kept when the queue overflows, and exempt from sequence deduplication MUST be decided only by `LocationUpdate.isCritical`, never by the text of its `status`; the flag stays with the sender and is not sent. The Sensor MUST set it only for an update carrying a violation (`JUMP`, `OUTLIER`), and MUST commit such an update to the forensic buffer at once. The Sensor MUST send `NO_FIX` with no position (latitude, longitude, and accuracy 0) on every tick while it has no fix, or while its newest fix is at least 15 seconds old (`FIX_STALE_AFTER_MS`, measured on `elapsedRealtimeNanos`), and MUST NOT send an older fix as current. It MUST NOT judge movement on, or keep as the reference, a fix whose accuracy is unknown or coarser than 100 m (`MOVEMENT_CHECK_MAX_ACCURACY_M`) (ISSUE-NOV7.19-O43, SIMP-NOV7.19-S1) (R-ID 147).
