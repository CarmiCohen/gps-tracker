# System Source of Truth (SoT) - Aug.18.08 (Diagnostic Stress Isolation)

This document serves as the definitive operational specification. All Issue IDs are Authoritative.

### 1. Performance & Startup Authority
*   **Forced LTR Layout Authority (R205)**: (Added Aug.18.08) Technical telemetry and numbering MUST always be rendered in Left-to-Right (LTR) direction to prevent BiDi (bidirectional) artifacts such as mirrored punctuation (e.g., ".1") or leading dots on wrapped lines. Technical overlays, including `PhoneSetupOverlay` and `TrackerDashboard`, MUST be wrapped in an LTR `CompositionLocalProvider` to ensure consistency across all locales. (Issue #205). **Status: Implemented.**
*   **Permission Navigation Hardening (R206)**: (Added Aug.18.08) The system MUST provide a robust path to system permission settings. If the specific `package:` URI intent is rejected by the OS (observed on certain Samsung/API 35 devices), the `MainActivity` MUST implement a fallback to a general settings list to ensure the user can manually authorize required capabilities. (Issue #206). **Status: Implemented.**
*   **Diagnostic Stress Isolation Authority (R204)**: (Added Aug.18.08) The system MUST support a temporary diagnostic down-sampling state to isolate the impact of high-frequency sensor processing and telemetry logging. Forensic sampling intervals MUST be reduced to 4Hz (`250ms`) and 2Hz (`500ms`). Hardware IMU listeners in `AppSensorManager` MUST utilize `SENSOR_DELAY_NORMAL` to reduce context-switching and I/O pressure during stress-test audits. (Issue #204). **Status: Implemented (Diagnostic).**
*   **Forensic Multi-Session Alignment Authority (R203)**: (Added Aug.18.07) The system MUST guarantee temporal monotonicity and zero-jitter continuity for forensic traces across service restarts and reboots. The `ForensicSpillBuffer` MUST store absolute `Long` timestamps and `Double` coordinates (v3) to eliminate overflow risks and session base-time dependencies. The `LogRepository` drainer MUST implement signature-based deduplication (timestamp + `spillIdx`) before database insertion to ensure idempotency during recovery from "dirty" shutdowns or crashes. (Issue #203). **Status: Implemented.**
*   **Forensic Allocation Optimization (R202)**: (Added Aug.18.06) The system MUST eliminate intermediate heap allocations during the forensic log draining process. The `ForensicSpillBuffer` MUST provide a direct mapping from the memory-mapped buffer to `LogEntity` objects (`peekToEntities`). The `LogRepository` drainer MUST utilize this zero-churn path to avoid the creation of intermediate `LogEntry` objects, thereby minimizing GC pressure during sustained 100Hz telemetry bursts. (Issue #202). **Status: Implemented.**
